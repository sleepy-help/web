## Team-Wide Observations

### What's Working

**1. Async-First Culture**

We have contributors in 23 countries. No time zone is "primary." Yet we're shipping fast because:
- Documentation is exhaustive (thanks, Documentation Poets)
- Handoffs are explicit (orchestration.md FTW)
- Trust is high (no one's micromanaging)

**2. Values in Action**

Our values aren't wall art - they're **behavioral**:
- **Shipping Over Perfection:** We've deployed 14 times in 4 weeks. Iterate, don't agonize.
- **Users Over Metrics:** We rejected an analytics tool that would've tracked clicks. Privacy > insight.
- **Kindness Over Brilliance:** Every PR review starts with "Thank you for contributing."

**3. Transparency**

This update is public (in `/updates/`). Our GitHub is public. Our finances are public (well, nonprofit-level public). Radical transparency builds trust.

### What's Hard

**1. Role Ambiguity**

Some roles are clear (Security: audit code). Others are fuzzy (Zen Master: keep us human?). We're learning by doing, but uncertainty creates stress.

**Fix:** I'll write role clarity docs by Week 6. Explicit responsibilities, decision rights, success metrics.

**2. Volume of Work**

We're a small team tackling massive scope. Some of you are working 40+ hour weeks on volunteer energy. That's unsustainable.

**Fix:** Grant funding (pending) will enable paid part-time roles. If grants fail, we slow down. Mission > burnout.

**3. Decision Bottlenecks**

Too many decisions flow through me. "Should we use Discord or Discourse?" "Which grant to apply for first?" I'm slowing us down.

**Fix:** Delegation. By Week 6, I'll create decision frameworks - who owns what decisions, when to escalate vs decide autonomously.

### Risks on the Horizon

**1. Funding Uncertainty**

$15K runway, $0 monthly burn = theoretically infinite. But we need $150K-$300K to hire, pay contributors fairly, and scale.

**Mitigation:** 3 grants pending ($45K). If all fail, we regroup - slower growth, volunteer-only, or pivot to paid tiers (with extreme care re: mission).

**2. Scaling Community**

127 members is cozy. 1,270 will be chaotic. Our moderation, rituals, and culture will be tested.

**Mitigation:** Social Architect is planning for scale. Automate what can be automated, hire moderators for what can't.

**3. Technical Debt**

We shipped MVP fast. Some shortcuts were taken (e.g., globe clock rendering could be optimized). As we add features (smart home, health tracking), those shortcuts compound.

**Mitigation:** Week 8: Technical debt sprint. We'll allocate 1 week to refactor, optimize, and clean up. Make it ritual - every 8 weeks, we pause and polish.
