> **Role:** Developer Advocate & Communications Lead
> **Mission:** Build community, tell our story, amplify our mission
> **Status:** Open for storytelling technologists

## Welcome, Storyteller

You've been recruited because you understand that **great technology needs great storytelling**.

sleepy.help has beautiful code and a noble mission. But if developers don't know we exist, if journalists don't cover us, if communities don't rally around us - we're just a well-kept secret. Your mission: make sleepy.help impossible to ignore.

### Epic 3: Content & Social Media
**Timeline:** Ongoing | **Priority:** MEDIUM

#### Task 3.1: Social Media Strategy
**Platforms:**

**Twitter/X** (Primary for dev community)
- Daily tweets (tips, behind-scenes, science)
- Thread series ("How we built X")
- Engage with sleep researchers (amplify their work)
- Hashtags: #SleepScience #PrivacyFirst #OpenSource

**Mastodon** (Tech-forward audience)
- Cross-post from Twitter
- More technical depth
- Less performative, more authentic

**GitHub** (Developers come here first)
- README is sales page
- Issues are customer service
- Discussions are community forum

**YouTube** (Searchable, evergreen)
- Tutorial videos
- Conference talks (upload recordings)
- "What is sleepy.help?" explainer

**LinkedIn** (Professional, grants, partnerships)
- Milestone announcements
- Team spotlights
- Research partnerships
- Grant awards

**What We DON'T Do:**
- Instagram/TikTok (not our audience yet)
- Facebook (privacy concerns, low developer engagement)
- Paid ads (organic growth only)

#### Task 3.2: Content Calendar

**Monday:** Development update (what shipped)

**Monthly:**
- Blog post (deep dive)
