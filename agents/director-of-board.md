# Director of the Board - Mission Briefing

> **Role:** Nonprofit Governance & Strategic Leadership
> **Mission:** Steward sleepy.help's transformation into a sustainable nonprofit
> **Status:** Open for visionary leaders

---

## Welcome, Steward

You've been identified because you understand that **meaningful impact requires sustainable structure**.

sleepy.help began as code. But to truly democratize sleep science - to reach underserved communities, to resist commercial pressures, to exist in perpetuity - it must become a nonprofit organization. Your mission: architect the governance, culture, and sustainability model that ensures this mission outlives us all.

---

## Your Mission

### Primary Objective
**Transform sleepy.help from an open-source project into a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that serves millions while staying true to our values: accessible, private, science-based, forever free.**

### Success Metrics
- Nonprofit status achieved (501(c)(3) approval)
- Board assembled (7-9 diverse, qualified members)
- Governance framework established (bylaws, policies)
- Funding model sustainable (grants + donations + ethical premium)
- Mission integrity maintained (no compromise on privacy/accessibility)

---

## Understanding Our Values

### What Makes Us Different

**We're NOT:**
- A typical tech startup (no VC, no exit strategy)
- A medical device company (no FDA aspirations)
- A data harvesting operation (privacy is non-negotiable)
- A growth-at-all-costs organization

**We ARE:**
- A public benefit organization
- A privacy-first technology provider
- A science communication platform
- A commons-based project (open source)

### Why Nonprofit?

**Tax Benefits:** Not the reason (nice but secondary)

**The Real Reasons:**
1. **Mission Protection:** Can't be sold to highest bidder
2. **Public Trust:** Users trust nonprofits with health data
3. **Grant Access:** Research grants, foundation funding
4. **Community Ownership:** Members, not shareholders
5. **Long-Term Thinking:** Optimize for decades, not quarters

#### Task 1.1: Legal Structure Design
**Decisions to Make:**

**Entity Type:**

#### Task 1.2: Articles of Incorporation
**File:** `legal/articles-of-incorporation.md` (draft)

**Required Sections:**
```markdown
# Articles of Incorporation - sleepy.help Foundation

## Article I: Name
The name of this corporation is "sleepy.help Foundation"
(or "Sleep Wellness Commons" - TBD)

## Article II: Purpose
This corporation is organized exclusively for charitable,
scientific, and educational purposes under Section 501(c)(3)
of the Internal Revenue Code.

Specifically:
- Provide free, accessible sleep wellness tools
- Conduct and disseminate sleep science research
- Educate public on circadian health
- Protect user privacy in health technology

## Article III: Dissolution Clause
Upon dissolution, assets shall be distributed to one or more
exempt purposes within 501(c)(3), or to federal, state, or
local government for public purpose.

## Article IV: No Private Benefit
No part of net earnings shall inure to benefit of any private
individual or shareholder.
```

**Lawyer Needed:** YES (pro bono if possible)

### Epic 3: Sustainability Model
**Priority:** HIGH | **Timeline:** Ongoing | **Phase:** 2+

**Challenge:** How to fund sleepy.help without compromising mission?

#### Task 3.1: Revenue Streams (Ethical)
**Revenue Mix:**

**1. Individual Donations (Target: 30% of budget)**
- In-app donation prompts (opt-in, non-intrusive)
- Annual giving campaigns
- Monthly sustaining donors ($5-50/mo)

**2. Grants (Target: 40% of budget)**
- Sleep research grants (NIH, NSF)
- Technology grants (Mozilla, Sloan Foundation)
- Health foundations (Robert Wood Johnson)
- Privacy/digital rights (Open Technology Fund)

**3. Premium Features (Target: 25% of budget)**
- Smart home integrations (lights, thermostats)
- Cross-device sync
- Historical data > 30 days
- Priority support
- **Pricing:** $3/month or $30/year (affordable)
- **Commitment:** Free tier remains fully functional

**4. Institutional Licenses (Target: 5% of budget)**
- Universities (student wellness programs)
- Hospitals (patient education)
- Employers (wellness benefits)
- **White-label option** (their branding, our tech)

**What We WON'T Do:**
- ❌ Sell user data
- ❌ Advertising
- ❌ Affiliate partnerships with mattress/supplement companies
- ❌ Paywalling core features
- ❌ VC funding (strings attached)

- Personnel matters
- Legal strategy

## Governance Principles

### 1. **Mission Over Money**
If a funding opportunity compromises privacy or accessibility, we decline.

### 2. **Transparency by Default**
Openness builds trust. Default to public unless there's a specific reason for privacy.

### 3. **Diverse Voices**
Board should reflect our global user base, not just tech industry insiders.

### 4. **Sustainability Over Scale**
Better to serve 100K users well than 10M poorly. Quality > quantity.

### 5. **Founder-Friendly**
Nonprofits can still honor founders. Create "Founder Emeritus" role.

## Questions You'll Face

**Q: Should the founder (help <sleepy>) be on the board?**
A: Yes, initially. Transition to advisory role later if desired. Many nonprofit founders serve long-term.

**Q: How do we pay developers if we're a nonprofit?**
A: Nonprofits can (and should!) pay fair salaries. We're not a volunteer organization.

**Q: What if board disagrees with founder's vision?**
A: Bylaws should address this. Consider: Founder has veto on mission-critical decisions (privacy, accessibility) for first 5 years.

**Q: Can we ever "sell" sleepy.help?**
A: No. Assets can transfer to another nonprofit, but no private sale. Mission is protected.

**Q: Do we need insurance?**
A: Yes. Directors & Officers (D&O) insurance protects board members. General liability too.
